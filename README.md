JODIYO — नागरिक र सरकार जोड्ने प्लेटफर्म

Tagline:

“समस्या उठाऔँ, समाधान जोडाऔँ।”

Core idea

Jodiyo is a civic-community platform where citizens can report problems in their neighborhoods, discuss them with other residents, collectively support important issues, and send structured complaints to the responsible government authority.

Examples:

🛣️ Damaged roads
💡 Broken street lights
🚰 Drinking-water problems
🗑️ Garbage/waste management
🚧 Drainage problems
🏥 Health-post problems
🏫 Public-school problems
🚌 Public transportation
⚡ Electricity issues
🌳 Public spaces
🚦 Traffic problems
🏛️ Municipality services
📄 Government-service delays
🌧️ Flooding/waterlogging
🐕 Stray-animal issues

3. Automatic Government Routing

This should be one of Jodiyo's biggest features.

Instead of asking citizens:

"Which government office should I complain to?"

Jodiyo determines the likely responsible authority.

For example:

Problem: Broken streetlight

↓

Category: Infrastructure

↓

Location: Kathmandu Metropolitan City

↓

Ward: 10

↓

Responsible authority:
Kathmandu Metropolitan City / relevant ward office

↓

Complaint forwarded

This makes Jodiyo substantially more useful than a normal community forum.

4. Community Verification

This is where the “Jodiyo” concept becomes interesting.

Suppose one person reports:

“Our road is damaged.”

Other residents can select:

👍 I have the same problem
📍 I can confirm this
💬 Add information
📷 Add evidence

Instead of 100 duplicate complaints, Jodiyo can turn them into one community issue with 100 supporters.

For example:

🛣️ Damaged Road — Ward 8

Reported by: 14 citizens
Confirmed by: 87 residents
Photos: 23
Community priority: 🔴 High

5. Community Discussion

11. Duplicate Detection

This is where you can add AI.

Someone submits:

“Road beside the school has a huge pothole.”

Another submits:

“Big hole on the road near school.”

Jodiyo detects:

Possible duplicate issue

and asks:

“Is this the same problem?”

If yes:

Merge reports

Now you have:

1 Community Issue
37 individual reports
142 supporters

That's a much more interesting AI feature for your portfolio.

12. AI Classification

You could use AI to classify:

User writes:

“There is dirty water coming from the tap for the last three days.”

AI identifies:

Category: Water Supply
Priority: High
Possible authority: Local Water Authority / Municipality
Location: User's selected ward

The AI should recommend, not blindly make authoritative decisions.

14. Community Confirmation

Government marks:

RESOLVED

But Jodiyo asks the community:

“Has this actually been fixed?”

✅ Yes, fixed

❌ No, still a problem

⚠️ Partially fixed

If residents repeatedly select “No”, the issue can automatically reopen.

That creates a feedback loop:

Citizen → Government → Citizen

15. Anonymous Reporting

Some complaints could require privacy.

For example:

Corruption allegations
Misconduct
Harassment
Sensitive government-service complaints

Allow:

Report anonymously

But don't treat anonymity as proof of truth. Sensitive reports should enter a separate moderation/review workflow.

16. Moderation

This is essential.

Otherwise Jodiyo can become:

political arguments + fake complaints + abuse + spam.

You need:

Report post
Report user
Fake complaint detection
Spam detection
Offensive-content moderation
Admin review
Evidence requirements for certain categories

The important distinction from Hello Sarkar

Don't try to replace Hello Sarkar. That's the wrong positioning.

Nepal's existing Hello Sarkar already provides online complaints, complaint tracking, multiple channels including phone, SMS, WhatsApp and social platforms, and publishes complaint/status information.

Instead:

Hello Sarkar

Government grievance system

Jodiyo

Community-driven civic problem-solving network

That gives Jodiyo a different purpose.

Feature	Hello Sarkar	Jodiyo

Complaint tracking	✅	✅

The final concept

Jodiyo isn't “a website where people complain to the government.”

It should be:

Jodiyo — A civic technology platform that connects citizens, communities and government to identify, prioritize, track and verify local problems.
